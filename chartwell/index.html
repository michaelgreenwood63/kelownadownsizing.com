Chartwell

A gated 55+ community of 85 rancher-style townhomes in Glenmore, built 1997.

Chartwell is a gated 55+ community at 680 Valley Road in Kelowna's North Glenmore neighbourhood, built in 1997 to 85 semi-detached, rancher-style townhomes. Units feature single-story, grade-level entry layouts — many with vaulted ceilings, fenced yards, patios, and double garages — with fee-based RV parking available on-site.

Worth clarifying: this Chartwell is an independently-owned strata townhome community — not connected to Chartwell Retirement Residences (the rental retirement-residence chain that operates Chartwell Chatsworth elsewhere in Kelowna). Same name, two entirely different kinds of housing — one you buy, one you rent. See our Senior Living Options page if it's the rental residence you're after.

Residents share a clubhouse with a gym, kitchen, and billiard room, and the location keeps you close to the Kelowna Golf & Country Club, shopping, and walking trails in one of Kelowna's more established residential pockets.

The Adderly (Adderley)

An affordable 55+ condo community in Rutland South — you'll see it spelled both 'Adderly' and 'Adderley.'

The Adderly — also commonly spelled "Adderley" — is a 55+ condo community at 920 Saskatoon Road in Rutland South, roughly 60 units of 1 and 2-bedroom homes. It's one of the more affordable entry points into 55+ ownership in the Kelowna area, with unit sizes generally in the 620-900 square foot range.

Confirm the tenure before shopping here specifically: some sources describe this community's fee structure as including pad rent / land lease elements alongside standard strata fees, which affects financing differently than a straightforward strata purchase — see our ownership types guide.

The Adderly (Adderley) — FAQ

Is it spelled Adderly or Adderley?
Both spellings are used for the same Rutland South community at 920 Saskatoon Road — worth searching both ways if you're looking for current listings elsewhere.
Is The Adderly affordable compared to other 55+ communities?
Generally yes — it's one of the more accessible entry points into 55+ ownership in the Kelowna area, with smaller unit sizes and correspondingly lower prices than many gated communities in Glenmore or Kelowna South.

Sandhaven

A gated 55+ community of 91 detached townhomes in Kelowna South, on the same road as Sandstone.

Sandhaven is a gated 55+ community of 91 detached townhomes at 1101 Cameron Avenue in Kelowna South — on the same road as, but a separate complex from, Sandstone (1201 Cameron Ave) nearby. It's one of the larger single-level communities in this part of the city, with an outdoor pool as a shared amenity.

Kelowna South's location keeps residents close to South Pandosy's shops and restaurants and Gyro Beach, in one of the city's more walkable, established neighbourhoods.

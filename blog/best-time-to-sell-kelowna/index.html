The Best Time of Year to Sell Your Home in Kelowna

July 2026 · By Mark Jontz & Associates · 6 min read

"Just wait for spring" is the default advice everyone gives, and it's not wrong — but it's not the whole picture either. Here's an honest, season-by-season look at Kelowna's market, including where spring's advantages come with real trade-offs worth knowing before you set a listing date.

Spring (mid-April through June): the most active season

Spring consistently brings the highest buyer demand and the fastest sales in Kelowna — homes typically move quickest and sellers tend to land closest to their asking price during this window. The catch: everyone else knows this too. Spring also brings the highest volume of new listings of any season, meaning your home is competing against the most other options on the market at the same time. Spring works well if your home shows well and is priced right — the demand is real — but it's not an automatic win just by virtue of the calendar.

Summer: high activity, high competition

Listing volume typically stays elevated through summer, with continued solid demand — including from people relocating to the Okanagan. It's a reasonable window, but similar to spring, you're not the only seller trying to capitalize on the season.

Fall (September-October): the underrated window

This is genuinely worth more attention than it usually gets. By September, a lot of the summer's listing volume has already sold or been pulled off the market, and active inventory starts declining from its peak — while serious, motivated buyers are still actively shopping. Less competition for your listing, without giving up much on genuine buyer interest, is a real advantage many sellers overlook.

Winter: lowest supply, most serious buyers

Winter brings the fewest new listings — which cuts both ways. Fewer buyers are actively shopping, but the ones who are tend to be serious, not casual browsers. If your home doesn't get lost in a crowded market, winter can mean standing out rather than competing.

What actually matters more than season, for most downsizers: your own readiness and the specific property you're moving into. A perfectly-timed spring listing doesn't help if you haven't sorted out where you're going next — which is exactly the problem our Homesafe program is built to solve, regardless of season.

The downsizing-specific timing question

For most home sales, "when's the market best" is the main question. For downsizing specifically, there's a second, often bigger question: is your target property — a specific 55+ community, a particular floor plan — likely to have inventory available when you're ready to buy? Some communities turn over rarely; watching specific communities over a few months before you're ready to list can matter more than picking the theoretically ideal season to sell.

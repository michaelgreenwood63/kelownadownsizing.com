Monticello

A 132-unit 55+ condo community in Westbank Centre, built 1996-1998, with a full clubhouse.

Monticello is a 55+ condo community at 3890 Brown Road in West Kelowna's Westbank Centre, built 1996-1998 across three low-rise buildings to 132 units. It's on the same road as Linden Estates (3854 Brown Road) — a separate, nearby complex worth not confusing with this one.

The amenity package here is substantial: a clubhouse with a games area, billiards room, lounge/library, full kitchen with wet bar, workshop, guest suites, communal garden plots, and RV/boat parking — one of the more complete offerings among West Kelowna's 55+ communities.

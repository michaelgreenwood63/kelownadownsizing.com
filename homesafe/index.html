Buy Your Next Home Before You Sell

Homesafe was built for exactly this situation — downsizers who don't want to gamble on timing.

Of every downsizing fear we hear, this is the biggest: what if I find the perfect single-level home or 55+ unit, but my current house hasn't sold yet? Most people assume the answer is to just sell first and hope something suitable turns up in time — or to take on a risky bridge loan and carry two mortgages. Homesafe exists because there's a better way to handle this exact situation.

How it works

  • You find and secure your next home first — no rushed decision, no settling because you're on the clock.
  • We coordinate both closings for the same day — your sale and your purchase complete together.
  • You move once — directly from your current home to your new one. No storage unit, no temporary rental, no second move.
  • No bridge financing required — you avoid carrying two mortgages while you wait for your old home to sell.

Why this matters more for downsizing than a typical move: downsizers are often moving into something specific and limited in supply — a particular 55+ community, a rare single-level rancher, a unit in a building you've had your eye on. Those don't come up constantly. Homesafe means you don't have to pass on the right home just because your current house hasn't sold yet.

What it takes

A same-day close isn't automatic — it requires coordinating your sale, your purchase, your lender, and your lawyer on the same timeline, ideally planned before your current home is even listed. This is exactly the kind of coordination our team handles regularly, and it's worth raising early in the process rather than after you've already found a home you love.

Honest limitations

  • It works best planned in advance — not after you've already accepted an offer on your current home and are searching under pressure.
  • Your financing still needs to support the purchase — Homesafe reduces timing risk, but doesn't remove the need for your numbers to work.
  • It's easiest to coordinate in a reasonably predictable market — very fast or very slow conditions can make exact timing harder to guarantee.

Sandalwood

A gated 55+ community of 141 detached homes in North Glenmore, on the same road as SandPointe.

Sandalwood is a gated 55+ community of 141 detached homes at 550 Yates Road in North Glenmore — on the same road as SandPointe (595 Yates Road), a distinct but nearby complex worth not confusing with this one. At 141 units, it's one of the larger age-restricted communities in the Kelowna area.

North Glenmore's established, central location keeps residents close to downtown Kelowna without the density of the urban core — one of several 55+ communities clustered in this part of the city alongside GlenMeadows and Regency Heights.

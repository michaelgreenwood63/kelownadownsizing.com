Downsizing in Peachland

A small, lake-hugging community popular with retirees for its slower pace and lower cost of living.

Peachland is a small municipality on the west shore of Okanagan Lake, south of West Kelowna, with a walkable downtown right on the water and a noticeably slower pace than Kelowna. It's long been popular with retirees specifically for that reason — small-town feel, lake views from many properties, and a generally lower cost of living than Kelowna proper.

The trade-off is distance: Peachland is further from Kelowna's hospital and larger shopping centres than West Kelowna is, typically 25-35 minutes depending on traffic. For downsizers who value quiet and affordability over quick access to a big city, that trade is often a welcome one — and Peachland's housing stock includes a meaningful number of single-level homes and condos suited to exactly that lifestyle.

Downsizing in Peachland — FAQ

Is Peachland good for retirees?
Yes — Peachland has long been popular with retirees for its small-town, walkable downtown, lake views, and lower cost of living compared to Kelowna, though it sits further from Kelowna's hospital and major shopping than West Kelowna does.
How far is Peachland from Kelowna?
Peachland is typically a 25-35 minute drive from central Kelowna, depending on traffic and the exact starting point.

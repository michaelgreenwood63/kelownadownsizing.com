Downsizing in Lake Country

Orchard country between Kelowna and Vernon — a quieter pace with growing single-level and retirement-oriented inventory.

Lake Country sits between Kelowna and Vernon, bordered by four lakes — Wood, Kalamalka, Ellison, and Okanagan — and rooted in orchard and vineyard agriculture that still defines much of its character today. It's smaller and quieter than Kelowna, with a genuine small-town feel despite being only about a 20-minute drive from central Kelowna and Kelowna International Airport.

Downsizers drawn to Lake Country tend to want the quieter pace without giving up easy access to a full-service city — Kelowna's hospital, shopping, and amenities are close by, while daily life in Lake Country itself moves at a noticeably calmer speed. Housing stock is more limited than Kelowna's, so working with a team that knows what's currently available matters more here than in a larger market.

Common Questions

Downsizing in Lake Country — FAQ

What is Lake Country known for?
Lake Country is known for its orchards, vineyards, and four surrounding lakes (Wood, Kalamalka, Ellison, and Okanagan) — a quieter, more rural feel than Kelowna while still being a short drive from the city.
Is Lake Country a good fit for downsizing?
It can be, especially for buyers who want a slower pace and don't mind a more limited selection of available properties compared to Kelowna. It's roughly 20 minutes from central Kelowna and the airport.

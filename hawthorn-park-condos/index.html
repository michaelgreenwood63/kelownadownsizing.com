Hawthorn Park Condos

197 independently-owned 55+ condos on the Hawthorn Park campus in Lower Mission, built 1991-1993.

Hawthorn Park Condos are independently-owned 55+ strata units spanning 877 KLO Road and 3157 Casorso Road in Lower Mission, built 1991-1993 to 197 units total across the campus. This is a real estate purchase like any other condo — worth distinguishing from the assisted living services also offered on the broader Hawthorn Park campus (see our Senior Living Options page for that distinction), which are a separate, rental-based care service, not something you buy.

Lower Mission's location keeps residents close to Gyro Beach and South Pandosy's dining, in one of Kelowna's most established, walkable neighbourhoods.

Hawthorn Park Condos — FAQ

Are Hawthorn Park Condos the same as Hawthorn Park assisted living?
They share a campus and name, but are different things: the condos at 877 KLO Road are independently owned strata units you buy like any condo; assisted living services on the same campus are a separate rental-based care service. See our Senior Living Options page for that distinction.

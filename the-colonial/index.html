The Colonial is a 55+ community — typically requiring at least one resident per unit to be 55 or older, with no children as permanent residents.
Is The Colonial a strata property?
Yes — Strata Condominium means you'd own your unit directly while sharing ownership of common areas through a strata corporation, which comes with monthly strata fees and bylaws. Confirm current fees, the depreciation report, and any pending special levies on any specific unit before writing an offer — standard practice for any strata purchase.

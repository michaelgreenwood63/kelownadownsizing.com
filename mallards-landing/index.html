Mallard's Landing

A 49-unit 55+ townhome community in Lower Mission, built 1998, with a full clubhouse.

Mallard's Landing is a 55+ strata townhome community at 3788 Bird Place in Lower Mission, built in 1998 to 49 units. It's one of the few age-restricted communities in Kelowna's most established lakeside neighbourhood, putting residents close to Gyro Beach and South Pandosy's restaurants and shops rather than in a more suburban setting.

The complex includes a well-appointed clubhouse — pool table, TV lounge, bar, swimming pool, whirlpool, and a fitness room — plus two guest suites for visiting family, a genuinely useful amenity for a smaller 49-unit community.

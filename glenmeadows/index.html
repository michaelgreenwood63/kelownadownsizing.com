GlenMeadows

A well-established, gated 55+ bare land strata community of 100 units in Glenmore, built 1991-1992.

GlenMeadows is a well-established 55+ gated community at 615 Glenmeadows Road in Kelowna's Glenmore neighbourhood, built out in 1991-1992 to 100 units on a bare land strata. Bare land strata means owners hold title to the land under their unit directly (rather than just an interior airspace), which is worth understanding as its own ownership variant alongside standard strata, freehold, co-op, and land-lease — see our ownership types guide for how this affects financing.

Its central Glenmore location keeps residents close to downtown Kelowna and the Kelowna Golf & Country Club, in one of the city's most established, quiet residential areas.

GlenMeadows — FAQ

What is bare land strata?
In a bare land strata, owners hold title to the land beneath their unit directly, rather than just the interior space — a distinct ownership variant from standard strata. Financing generally works similarly to standard strata, but confirm with your lender.

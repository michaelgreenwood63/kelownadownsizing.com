Orchard Place 1

A 48-unit 55+ community on Parkview Crescent, built in 1993.

Orchard Place 1 is a 55+ strata community of 48 units at 1874 Parkview Crescent, built in 1993. It's one of two confirmed 55+ communities on this stretch of Parkview Crescent, alongside the smaller Maples at 1800 Parkview Crescent — worth touring both if you're shopping this specific street, since they differ meaningfully in size and management style.

Note that not everything on Parkview Crescent is age-restricted: nearby buildings on the same street include family-oriented, non-age-restricted options, so always confirm the exact civic number before assuming a listing on this street is 55+.

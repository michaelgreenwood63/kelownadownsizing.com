Cadence at The Lakes

Maintenance-free rancher-style homes for 45+ buyers in Lake Country, about 15 minutes from Kelowna.

Cadence at The Lakes is a 45+ active-adult community in Lake Country, roughly 15 minutes from Kelowna, built around maintenance-free rancher-style homes with open-concept floor plans ranging from about 1,250 to 3,200 square feet — a notably wide range, from compact to genuinely spacious. Homes feature fully landscaped yards and geothermal heating/cooling, and new construction has been priced from roughly $419,900 to $599,900.

For buyers drawn to Lake Country's quieter, orchard-country pace but who still want a purpose-built, maintenance-free single-level home rather than an older resale property, Cadence is one of the newer options in the area.

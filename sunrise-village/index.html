Sunrise Village

A large, centrally-located 45+ manufactured home community in Kelowna's Springfield/Spall area.

Sunrise Village is one of Kelowna's largest age-restricted communities — a 45+ manufactured and mobile home park on Raymer Avenue in the Springfield/Spall area, central enough to be close to Orchard Park Mall and most of Kelowna's everyday amenities. It's not a strata condo building and not assisted living — it's a community of standalone manufactured homes.

Ownership here is worth understanding before you shop: some homes are on land lease (you own the home outright but lease the pad it sits on from the park), while tenure can vary by specific unit. This matters for financing — land-lease properties are financed differently than a typical strata or freehold purchase, so confirm the tenure on any specific unit early in the process.

Sunrise Village — FAQ

Is Sunrise Village a strata community?
No — Sunrise Village is a manufactured/mobile home park, not a strata condo building. Some homes are on land-lease tenure rather than owned freehold land, which affects financing.
What age restriction applies at Sunrise Village?
Sunrise Village is a 45+ adult-oriented community.

55+ & 45+ Communities

Age-restricted living across Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country & Peachland — quieter streets, single-level living, and neighbours in a similar life stage.

A 55+ (or 45+) community is a strata development with an age-restriction bylaw — usually requiring at least one resident per unit to be above the stated age, with no children living there permanently. They're popular with downsizers for the predictability: no loud teenagers next door, generally well-kept common areas, and often a built-in social calendar.

MLS® data doesn't have a standard "55+" field, so the live listings below are matched by searching each description for age-restriction language. It will miss some genuine 55+ listings whose description didn't happen to mention it — contact us for a complete, manually-checked list for any specific building.

Every community below was individually verified — real address, real age restriction, cross-checked against current MLS® activity — by the Mark Jontz & Associates team at Royal LePage Kelowna, not pulled wholesale from a single source. Where sources conflicted or we couldn't confirm a detail, we said so on that community's page rather than guessing.

Common Questions

55+ Communities — FAQ

What's the difference between 45+ and 55+ communities?
The number refers to the minimum age required for at least one resident per unit under the strata's bylaws. 45+ communities are less common and slightly more flexible than 55+, but both restrict permanent residency by children. Always confirm the exact bylaw wording for a specific building — they vary.
Can my adult children or grandchildren visit?
Yes — age restrictions apply to permanent residency, not visitors. Most 55+ bylaws allow guests to stay for a defined period (often a set number of days per year), which varies by strata. Confirm the specific bylaw before assuming.
Are pets allowed in 55+ communities?
It depends entirely on the individual strata's bylaws — some allow pets with size/number restrictions, others don't. This is one of the details worth confirming before you fall in love with a specific unit.
Do 55+ communities have extra fees?
Like any strata, 55+ buildings charge monthly strata fees that vary by building — often somewhat higher than a standard condo if the community includes extra amenities (clubhouse, activities, landscaping). Fees are building-specific; there's no single number that applies across all of them.

Ask Yourself

Is it time to downsize?

There's no single right age or moment — but these are the signs we hear about most often from Okanagan homeowners who end up glad they made the move.

"You want to move out of your own volition."— Mark Jontz, on the difference between downsizing by choice and downsizing by crisis
01

Rooms you rarely use

If half the house exists just for guests who visit twice a year, you're paying to heat, clean, and maintain space you don't need.

02

Stairs are getting harder

A two-storey home that made sense at 45 can become a genuine daily obstacle at 70. A single-level home removes that problem entirely.

03

Maintenance feels like a burden, not a hobby

Yard work, gutters, roof repairs — if these have gone from satisfying to stressful, a lower-maintenance property changes your relationship with your home.

04

You want to unlock equity

Many Okanagan homeowners are sitting on significant home equity. Downsizing can free up capital for retirement, travel, or helping family — without changing your lifestyle much at all.

05

You're thinking about community

55+ communities offer a built-in social environment and neighbours in a similar life stage — something a family-oriented neighbourhood often doesn't.

06

You want less to worry about, period

Less house means less to insure, less to repair, less to think about — freeing up time and mental energy for the things you'd rather be doing.

Common Questions

Downsizing in Kelowna — FAQ

What does "downsizing" actually mean?
Downsizing means moving from a larger home to a smaller, lower-maintenance property — typically a condo, rancher, bungalow, or townhome — often to reduce upkeep, unlock home equity, or simplify life in retirement. It doesn't have to mean giving up quality of life; many downsizers end up happier with less space to manage.
Should I sell my current home before I buy a new one?
You don't have to choose blindly — this is one of the most common downsizing fears, and it has a real solution. Our Homesafe program lists your home with a "subject to finding another suitable property" clause, so you have zero obligation to sell until you've actually secured your next place, whether that's a purchase, a retirement residence, or a rental — with both moves coordinated for the same day.
What is a 55+ or 45+ community?
A 55+ (or 45+) community is a strata development with an age-restriction bylaw — typically requiring at least one resident per unit to be above the stated age, with no children as permanent residents. They're popular for the quieter, more predictable environment and neighbours in a similar life stage. See our 55+ Communities page for current listings.
What's the difference between a rancher and a bungalow?
In everyday BC real estate use, the terms are largely interchangeable — both describe a single-story detached home with no stairs to the main living area. Some ranchers have a basement below (still single-level living upstairs), while true bungalows are typically slab-on-grade with no basement at all.
How much does it cost to downsize in Kelowna?
It depends entirely on your current home's value versus your target property, plus selling costs (typically ~5% in realtor commission and legal fees) and moving costs. For many Okanagan homeowners who've owned for a decade or more, downsizing actually releases a meaningful amount of equity rather than costing money outright. Contact us for a free, no-obligation estimate specific to your situation.
What should I do with belongings I don't need anymore?
Most successful downsizers sort belongings into four categories early: keep, sell, donate, and family heirlooms to pass on now rather than later. Starting this process months before you list — not the week before your move — makes the biggest difference in how stressful the process feels. Our free downsizing guide walks through this step by step.
Do I need a REALTOR® who specializes in downsizing?
You don't strictly need one, but it helps — downsizing involves different considerations than a typical move (timing two transactions, understanding 55+ strata bylaws, matching lifestyle needs to property type) that a generalist agent may not think to raise.

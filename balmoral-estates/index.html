Balmoral Estates

A gated 55+ strata community of roughly 250 homes, built 2003-2008 in Kelowna South.

If you're considering Balmoral Estates, you're probably weighing the same things most downsizers do: will I actually like living somewhere smaller, will I know anyone, and is this the right trade-off for the equity I'd be unlocking. Here's what actually matters about this one.

Balmoral Estates is a gated 55+ community on Stillingfleet Road in Kelowna South, built out in phases between 2003 and 2008 to roughly 250 homes. Because it's a single, fairly large, same-era development, it has a consistent look and feel — and a genuine sense of neighbours who are in a similar stage of life, which is often what people find they miss most about a typical family neighbourhood once the kids are grown. Being one consistent era of construction also means building systems (roofs, mechanical) across the complex are on a broadly similar age and replacement timeline — worth asking about in any specific unit's depreciation report.

Its Kelowna South location puts it within walking distance of South Pandosy's shops, cafés, and restaurants, and close to Gyro Beach, while the gated, age-restricted setting keeps it quieter than the surrounding neighbourhood — close enough to stay connected to the city, private enough to actually feel like a change of pace.

And if the thing holding you back isn't the decision but the move itself — packing up a house's worth of belongings — we can connect you with trusted local packing, moving, and unpacking help, so that part doesn't have to fall entirely on you.

Common Questions

Balmoral Estates — FAQ

When was Balmoral Estates built?
Balmoral Estates was developed in phases between 2003 and 2008, to a total of roughly 250 homes.
Is Balmoral Estates gated?
Yes — Balmoral Estates is a gated 55+ community in Kelowna South.
What are the strata fees, and what do they cover?
Strata fees at Balmoral Estates were around $263/month as of 2024 (confirm the current figure on any specific unit — it varies slightly by size). That covers the contingency reserve, management, road maintenance, snow and trash removal, and use of the clubhouse — which includes a pool, hot tub, and exercise room.
Are pets allowed?
Yes, with restrictions — pets under roughly 18" are generally welcome, which covers most small-to-medium dogs and cats. Always worth confirming current bylaws on the specific unit you're considering, since strata rules can be amended.
Will I actually know anyone here?
It's one of the more common hesitations we hear, and it's a fair one. Balmoral's clubhouse (pool, hot tub, exercise room) tends to be where that happens naturally — being one consistent age-restricted community of 250 homes means your neighbours are genuinely in a similar stage of life, which is different from a typical family-oriented street.

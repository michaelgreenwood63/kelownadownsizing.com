The Complete Downsizing Guide

Written specifically for Kelowna and Okanagan homeowners — free, no obligation, no email wall.

Downsizing touches more decisions than a typical move — timing your sale against your purchase, understanding what your equity actually gives you to work with, deciding what to do with decades of belongings, and choosing a next home that actually fits your life now rather than the life you had 20 years ago. Our free guide walks through each of these in detail.

What's inside

  • Is it time to downsize? A real framework for deciding, not just a birthday.
  • Step 1 & 2 — the actual process, including budgeting and setting a realistic timeline.
  • The true costs of selling — commission, legal fees, and other costs people often forget to budget for.
  • Net proceeds — a real walkthrough of what you'd actually walk away with after selling.
  • What to do with your belongings — a practical system for sorting what comes with you.
  • Choosing your next home — rancher vs. condo vs. 55+ community, matched to your lifestyle.
  • The benefits of downsizing most people don't think about until after they've done it.

No email required, no sales pressure. Read it, download it, or print it — whatever's useful to you. If you have questions after reading, we're one call away.

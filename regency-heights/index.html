Regency Heights

A gated 55+ community of 2-4 bedroom townhomes in North Glenmore, on the same road as its neighbour GlenMeadows.

Regency Heights is a gated 55+ community at 555 Glenmeadows Road in North Glenmore — on the same road as, but a distinct complex from, its neighbour GlenMeadows at 615 Glenmeadows Road. Worth double-checking the exact address when searching, since the similar naming and shared street cause mix-ups between the two.

Units here run larger than many other 55+ communities in the area, with 2 to 4 bedroom layouts — a genuine option for downsizers who still want room for visiting family rather than a strictly compact unit.

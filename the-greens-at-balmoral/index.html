The Greens at Balmoral

A 46-home gated 55+ community near Guisachan Village — a distinct complex from Balmoral Estates despite the similar name.

The Greens at Balmoral is a gated 55+ community of 46 single-family homes and detached townhomes at 1405 Guisachan Place, near Guisachan Village in the Springfield/Spall area. Despite the shared "Balmoral" name and nearby location, it's a genuinely separate development from Balmoral Estates (2365 Stillingfleet Road) — worth confirming which one you're actually looking at before shopping.

At just 46 homes, it's a smaller, quieter community than some of its larger neighbours, in one of Kelowna's more established, central pockets.

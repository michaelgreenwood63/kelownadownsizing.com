Sandstone

A gated 55+ community off Gordon Drive near Guisachan Village.

Sandstone is a gated 55+ strata community on Cameron Avenue, in a green, established pocket near Guisachan Village and Gordon Drive. It's centrally located relative to Kelowna's core, putting shopping, medical offices, and South Pandosy's restaurants within easy reach without living directly in the busier parts of town.

Like most of Kelowna's gated 55+ strata communities, unit styles here range from ranchers to townhomes — worth touring a few different unit types within the same complex, since layouts and outdoor space can vary meaningfully even within one development.
